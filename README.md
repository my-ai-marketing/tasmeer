# Tasmeer Indigo — Marketing & CRM Transformation Strategy

Executive interview dashboard for building Tasmeer Indigo's marketing, CRM, attribution and growth function from the ground up.

## Visual system
- Theme: James Realty — deep navy, indigo and violet.
- Display type: Roboto Flex, expanded width, matching the Mudon landing-page typography system.
- Body/UI type: Manrope.
- Fonts are loaded at runtime; no font files are stored in this repository.

## Dashboard coverage
- Current digital presence
- Brand and conversion gaps
- Positioning strategy
- Full-funnel acquisition
- CRM and attribution architecture
- CRM shortlist
- Paid-media mix
- Hiring and vendor model
- 30/60/90-day roadmap
- KPI and executive reporting framework
- Research sources

## Presentation note
Directional audit scores and recommended channel allocations are strategic recommendations, not internal Tasmeer performance data. Absolute media budgets and commercial KPI targets should be finalized after access to inventory economics, sales targets and historical lead/conversion data.
