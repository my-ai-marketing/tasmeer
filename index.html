03 / Competitive position

Do not benchmark only against giants.
Benchmark against the buyer’s alternatives.

The useful competitive set is the group of developers competing for similar JVC and investor demand. This is a strategic benchmark set, not a ranking.

Binghatti
Brand salience + launch velocity

Strong visibility, broad distribution and frequent project communication raise the standard for awareness.

Danube
Payment-plan communication

Commercial offers are made easy to understand, giving buyers and brokers a clear reason to act.

Imtiaz
Product + amenity presentation

Visually strong property storytelling increases pressure on product presentation and lifestyle proof.

Object 1
Investor-oriented JVC product

Competes for similar apartment-led demand, making differentiation and broker mindshare important.

Ellington
Design + quality perception

Sets a high reference point for design-led positioning and confidence-building product detail.

Tasmeer opportunity
Agility + proof + response speed

Win by being easier to trust, easier for brokers to sell and faster at converting qualified buyer intent.

Win condition 01

Proof

Construction, escrow, availability, payment plan and management visibility must be easy to verify.

Win condition 02

Offer clarity

The buyer should understand price logic, payment structure and ownership case within minutes.

Win condition 03

Broker ease

Inventory, assets, training, lead registration and commission information should be frictionless.

Win condition 04

Sales speed

Qualified demand loses value quickly if the sales follow-up system is inconsistent.

08 / Broker growth strategy

09 / 90-day growth plan

Prove first.
Scale second.

Days 1-30

Prove

  • Reconcile project, inventory, price, payment plan and handover data.
  • Establish CRM lead stages, source attribution and sales response baseline.
  • Launch the AED 100K validation plan by priority market.
  • Benchmark buyer objections, competitor offers and broker feedback.
  • Define the CEO scorecard and weekly commercial review.
Days 31-60

Build

  • Concentrate spend on markets producing qualified viewing intent.
  • Launch recurring trust, product and investor education content.
  • Run broker training and activation as a measured sales programme.
  • Improve landing pages, WhatsApp, call tracking and sales routing.
  • Start feeding qualified and booking outcomes back into media optimisation.
Days 61-90

Scale

  • Release additional budget only where booking economics are visible.
  • Cut sources that remain cheap but low quality.
  • Expand winning markets, creative territories and broker cohorts.
  • Report spend against pipeline, bookings, booked value and sell-through.
  • Present the next-quarter investment case using actual Tasmeer data.

11 / Risks & management decisions

Growth depends on decisions outside marketing.

These are management dependencies that can materially improve or damage marketing economics.

High

Inventory data mismatch

Different public portfolio views create confusion. Approve one project master dataset across web, CRM, portals and brokers.

CEO decision: owner + deadline
High

Slow sales response

Marketing cannot recover value from qualified demand if ownership and follow-up are inconsistent.

CEO decision: SLA + escalation
Medium

Offer competitiveness

Price, payment plan and available inventory must remain competitive against immediate buyer alternatives.

CEO decision: review cadence
Medium

Trust proof

Construction progress, project facts, leadership visibility and ownership clarity should be continuously published.

CEO decision: proof standard
Medium

Broker inactivity

Registrations have limited value unless brokers are trained, activated and producing opportunities.

CEO decision: channel ownership
High

Budget scaling too early

Large spend before booking economics are visible can amplify low-quality demand.

CEO decision: release gates
12 / Execution model

The technical system sits under the commercial plan.

This is where the marketing team operates. The CEO only needs the outputs unless a deeper operational review is required.

01

Brand & conversion

Project proof, investor education, CRO, landing pages, content governance and market-specific messaging.

02

Demand generation

Google, Meta, portals, video, retargeting and market testing with budget controlled by commercial intent.

03

CRM & attribution

One source of truth from enquiry to qualification, viewing, EOI, booking and SPA.

04

Sales operations

Routing, lead ownership, response SLA, reassignment, lost reasons and offline conversion feedback.

05

Broker enablement

Registration, training, inventory, campaign kits, lead registration and booking attribution.

06

Team & vendors

Keep strategy, data and account ownership inside. Rent specialist production and implementation capacity.
