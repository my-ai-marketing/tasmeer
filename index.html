03 / Gaps & risks

The gaps are mostly systemic.

Fix the operating system before aggressively scaling lead volume.

01

Website hierarchy needs tighter governance

The public site has repeated content patterns and can be reorganised around clearer project discovery, investor proof and conversion actions.

02

Lead capture should qualify, not only collect contact details

Budget, purchase timeline, investor/end-user intent, unit preference, country, language and project interest should flow into CRM from the first interaction.

03

Brand language is too category-generic

Luxury, innovation and elegance are valid but crowded claims. Tasmeer needs a sharper position supported by product proof.

04

Owned search authority can be stronger

Build authority around JVC, off-plan ownership, investor education, project progress, payment plans and Tasmeer’s own projects.

05

Broker onboarding can become a growth platform

Turn registration into a CRM-connected channel-partner engine with live inventory, project training, campaign kits, lead registration and commission visibility.

06

Leadership content is under-leveraged

Existing management interviews and PR can become a recurring trust pillar covering development philosophy, project progress, market insight and buyer education.

04 / Brand strategy

Do not become a smaller version of a larger developer.

Position Tasmeer around design-led Dubai property with strong lifestyle value and investor fundamentals.

Brand pillar 01
Design

Architecture, layouts, interiors, finishing, smart-home experience and amenity quality. Show evidence, not adjectives.

Brand pillar 02
Lifestyle

Wellness, co-working, recovery, leisure, fitness, social space and JVC convenience translated into lived experience.

Brand pillar 03
Value

Entry price, payment structure, location fundamentals, rental-demand story, buyer education and long-term ownership logic.

Positioning principle

Proof before promotion.

Construction progress, project data, management visibility, transparent availability and buyer education should build as much trust as premium renders.

09 / 30–60–90 day roadmap

Foundation first.
Scale second.

Days 1–30

Foundation

  • Audit website, analytics, ad accounts, portals, social, broker database, lead sources and sales process.
  • Create project master data: inventory, price, payment plan, handover, approved claims and contact details.
  • Run CRM proof-of-concept against lead → qualification → viewing → EOI → booking workflow.
  • Define UTM taxonomy, lead statuses, routing, qualification, SLA and lost reasons.
  • Fix critical conversion and content-governance issues before traffic scaling.
Days 31–60

Build

  • CRM go-live with lead routing, WhatsApp, telephony and dashboards.
  • Launch project-specific landing pages and full event tracking.
  • Start controlled Google, Meta and retargeting tests by priority market.
  • Launch broker enablement cadence: training, campaign kits, inventory and lead registration.
  • Begin SEO clusters and leadership / construction trust content.
Days 61–90

Scale

  • Shift budget from cheap leads toward high-quality channels, markets and creatives.
  • Import qualified-lead, viewing and booking outcomes back to advertising platforms.
  • Score sales follow-up performance and enforce reassignment rules.
  • Expand winning search and market-specific creative structures.
  • Move management reporting from campaign metrics to pipeline and revenue.

11 / Interview close

The goal is not a marketing department.
It is a revenue operating system.

“Tasmeer already has the assets to grow: projects, a product story, digital touchpoints and broker relationships. My first 90 days would connect those assets through clean data, CRM discipline, conversion-focused digital experiences and a team built around measurable commercial outcomes.”
1

Out-operate

Do not try to outspend category giants. Win through response speed, attribution, broker activation and creative learning.

2

Own the data

Agencies can execute. Tasmeer must own the accounts, audiences, tracking, CRM and customer intelligence.

3

Scale proof

Move budget only after proving which market, channel, creative and sales motion creates qualified pipeline and bookings.
